October 10, 2025 · Devoxx · 67 min

The Future of Refactoring: Test-Driven Navigation

AI promised to take refactoring off our hands and instead delivers sweeping, uncontrolled changes. Test-Driven Navigation is the workflow that tames it: failing tests act as hyper-specific instructions that force the assistant into safe, incremental steps. Combines XP's three C's with Permaculture by Succession, includes a live-coded red-green-refactor cycle with an AI partner, and reframes code review — writing the next test is the review. The closing argument is that the developer's role is not obsolete but elevated: mechanic to race engineer, coder to architect.
